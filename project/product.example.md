# The product (example: replace with your own and save as product.md)

## What it is, in one sentence
An app that reminds small business owners to send invoices on time, and sends them for them.

## Who it is for
- **Buyer:** the business owner.
- **User:** the owner or their bookkeeper.

## The pains (real situations, not features)
1. End of the month: dozens of unsent invoices, and a client calling to ask where theirs is.
2. Money stuck because nobody sent a reminder.
3. A whole evening of copy-pasting from a spreadsheet.

## What works today, and what is planned
- **Today:** automatic sending, reminders.
- **Planned:** bank connection.
